💡 Cameo Specs & Sensor Trick (ProxyJak 330gsm)

Find the force, speed and passes with a small test cut on your Cameo 4 and ProxyJak stock. Start with one pass and inspect without unloading the mat before repeating.

The Silhouette Card Maker guide provides Cameo 5 reference settings and explains how to tune them. Your Cameo 4 needs its own test.

Optical sensor masking trick

On Letter landscape, card #5 sits ~1.2 mm inside the sensor search margin. Place a small sticky note (Post-It) over the bottom-left card artwork during registration scanning. Peel it off once marks are read, then cut!

  1. Cut guides: Automatically set to None so rotary lines aren't printed.
  2. Bleed: 0.625 mm with 0 mm gap matches standard Silhouette Card Maker spacing (1.25 mm between cards).
  3. Silhouette Studio: Match the page and registration settings listed in step 4, Export.

💡 Trimmer Tips & Stop-Block Technique
Standard MTG Proxy Specs (Eliminate White Edges)

• Trim size (finished card): 2.5″ × 3.5″ (63 × 88 mm)
• Standard bleed: 0.125″ (3 mm) on each side
• File size with bleed: 2.75″ × 3.75″ (69 × 94 mm)
A 2–3 mm bleed creates a clean ~4–6 mm strip between cards. This gives your rotary trimmer blade enough paper thickness to shear cleanly without tearing, and absorbs 0.5–1 mm of normal cutting drift so white edges never appear.

Uniform Sizing Trick: Stop-Block Technique

Don't measure cards by eye! Build a physical stop on your trimmer fence: stack painter's tape or clamp a block at 88 mm (strip cross-cut) and 63 mm (card width). Slide paper against the stop block before each cut for 0.1 mm precision across your deck.

  • 2–3 mm bleed: Gives the trimmer blade enough paper thickness to shear cleanly without tearing, and prevents white slivers from normal cut drift.
  • Margin ticks: Guide outer cuts so margins drop off cleanly.
  • Corner marks: Remain visible on strips after margins are gone to guide cross-cuts.
  • Kadomaru Pro: Use the 3 mm slot on single cards face-up for authentic corner radius.
