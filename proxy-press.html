A PDF with the registration marks in the same place as on your print, and a faint outline of every card position: a thin solid line at the bleed edge and a dashed line at the safe area. The outlines are pure black, so a colour laser prints them from black toner alone. Print it on scrap paper and cut it. If the cutter finds the marks and the cuts are centred, each cut falls between the two lines with an even margin all round.
