# Proxy Press v0.10.4

- Clicking **Back** in Sheet Preview now stays enabled whenever a deck has cards and shows a clear **No card backs yet** prompt with direct **Browse cardbacks…** and **Upload back…** actions.
- **Browse cardbacks…** and **Upload back…** are now always visible under **Print settings → Layout → Card backs**, and choosing a cardback automatically enables **Print a shared back**.
